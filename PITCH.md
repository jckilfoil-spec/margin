# PITCH.md

The soul of **[PROJECT_NAME]**. Two minutes to read. Referenced by agents,
designers, and the council to understand WHY before touching anything.

> This is not the business plan — that's STRATEGY.md.
> This is the emotional north star. Keep it short. Keep it human.

---

## What it is

_One sentence. Plain language. No tech, no jargon._
_Example: "A calm place where Quinn can draw spider webs and feel proud of what she made."_

---

## Who it's for

_One real person. Name them if you can._
_Example: "Quinn, almost 4. She wants to make things, not be taught. She gets bored in 90 seconds if something isn't responding to her. She lights up when her mom says 'whoa, you made that?'"_

---

## What it feels like

_Describe the experience, not the features. What emotion does a great session produce?_
_Example: "Quiet focus. Like coloring, but the colors move. No wrong moves. Every web looks beautiful."_

---

## What winning looks like for the user

_One outcome. The thing they walk away with — not a metric, a feeling or a capability._
_Example: "Quinn shows her dad the web she made and says 'I made that.' He saves a screenshot."_

---

## What it is NOT

_The scope limit, stated in human terms. Prevents the product from becoming everything._
_Example: "It's not a game. There's no score, no timer, no failure state. The moment it feels like school, we've lost."_

---

## The one-line version

_The sentence you'd say at a dinner party if someone asked what you're building._

> "[One sentence that makes someone say 'oh that sounds cool' or 'my kid would love that.']"

---

_Update this file when the product's soul shifts — not when features change.
If you can't write this file, you don't know what you're building yet. Write it first._
